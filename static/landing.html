FAQ

Good questions.

Does the AI decide which leads are hot?

No. The AI only reads the message and fills in the facts. The score comes from fixed rules you can see above, and you make the final call.

Does LeadScout send messages to my leads?

Never. It drafts a reply for you to copy, edit and send yourself.

What if the AI is unavailable?

Built in rules take over automatically and each result is labelled, so you always know what read the message. The scoring rules don't change.

Can I upload many leads at once?

Yes, a CSV with a message column (and optionally name), up to 50 leads per file. Results are ranked and can be exported.

Where is my data stored?

Leads you analyze are saved to your account so you can find them again, and only you can see them. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does it work for Indian and US markets?

Yes. It understands rupees with lakhs and crores as well as dollars, and phrases like 3BHK or 3 bed.
